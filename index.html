Questions

What does Re-Audit do?

It reads the degree audit PDF you save from TSS (the Triton Student System) next to your My Academic History, and matches every class to your major, college and university requirements. Each requirement is marked Done, In progress, Ask to apply, Petition or Still needed, and anything unclear becomes a short, specific question you can send to your advisor through the Virtual Advising Center.

Is my audit or academic history uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. There are no accounts and no server that receives your records, and Re-Audit doesn't use AI.

Is this the official UCSD degree audit?

No. Re-Audit is an unofficial student project and isn't affiliated with UC San Diego. The official degree audit and your advisors have the final word. Whenever the official audit shows a requirement as met, Re-Audit goes with the audit.

Which majors and colleges does it cover?

23 majors, including Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, Data Science, Mathematics, Biology, Cognitive Science, Psychology, Economics, Political Science, Communication and Sociology. It also checks Sixth College general education and university requirements (ELWR, AHI, DEI, total and upper-division units), and suggests UC Santa Cruz transfer classes that may count. If your major isn't covered yet, it still checks everything else.

Why would my degree audit be missing classes?

Classes sometimes show up on your academic history before they're applied to a requirement on the audit, especially transfer courses and classes that need an advisor to apply them by hand. Re-Audit points those out so you can ask advising to apply them or file a petition.
